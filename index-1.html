Privacy Policy for Jugaad Jam

Last updated: October 2026

Jugaad Jam ("the app") is a puzzle game developed by Khushal Suthar. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores your chosen player name, high score, game level progress and sound/music settings locally on your device. This data is not sent to our servers. We do not operate any servers that collect your personal data. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions to show and measure ads. You can learn more at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

3. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only with Google AdMob as described above.

5. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
